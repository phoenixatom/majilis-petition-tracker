# Font sources

`mv-faseyha.woff2` is the MV Faseyha webfont used by Mihaaru and was retrieved
from its public production stylesheet asset. Thaana Font Gallery lists MV Faseyha
by Hassan Hameed as free for personal and commercial use.

- Mihaaru: https://mihaaru.com/
- Font reference: https://thaanafontgallery.com/fonts/
